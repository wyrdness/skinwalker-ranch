## Overview

Skinwalker Ranch is a property in Utah that claims to be the site of extensive paranormal activity including UFO sightings, cattle mutilations, poltergeist phenomena, and cryptid encounters. The ranch gained attention during the Sherman family's ownership (1994-1996) and was subsequently purchased by billionaire Robert Bigelow's National Institute for Discovery Science (NIDS) from 1996-2004.

The property has connections to the Pentagon's AAWSAP (Advanced Aerospace Weapon System Applications Program). Despite claims of intense investigation, **no peer-reviewed scientific evidence has been published** substantiating the paranormal claims.

The ranch is now owned by Adamantium Real Estate and is the subject of a History Channel reality TV series. Critics view it as commercial exploitation with little credible evidence, while proponents claim genuine unexplained phenomena occur there.
